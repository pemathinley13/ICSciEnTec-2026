Welcome to ICSciEnTec
Science · Engineering · Technology

ICSciEnTec is an annual international conference jointly organised by the IEEE R10 Bhutan Subsection and the College of Science and Technology (CST), Royal University of Bhutan. The conference serves as a platform for bringing together academics, industry leaders, researchers, and professionals from around the world to foster innovation, advance research excellence, and strengthen collaboration in the fields of science, engineering, and technology.
