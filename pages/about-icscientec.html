About ICSciEnTec

International Conference on Science, Engineering & Technology

The International Conference on Science, Engineering and Technology (ICSciEnTec) is an annual international conference dedicated to advancing research, innovation, and knowledge exchange in science, engineering, and emerging technologies. The conference provides a dynamic platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, policymakers, and innovators from around the world to share ideas, present research findings, and explore solutions to contemporary global challenges.

Organized by the IEEE R10 Bhutan Subsection, the College of Science and Technology (CST), Royal University of Bhutan, and Construction Development Corporation Limited (CDCL), ICSciEnTec promotes interdisciplinary collaboration and strengthens the connection between academia, industry, and technological advancement. Through keynote addresses, technical sessions, paper presentations, and networking opportunities, ICSciEnTec fosters meaningful dialogue, encourages innovative thinking, and contributes to sustainable scientific and technological development.

Purpose & Audience

Objectives & Who Should Attend

Conference Objectives

  • Provide a global platform for sharing research and innovation in engineering, science, and technology.
  • Promote interdisciplinary collaboration and strengthen academic and industry partnerships.
  • Encourage knowledge exchange, research excellence, and sustainable technological advancement.
  • Enable CST to establish research linkages and collaborations with universities and agencies in science, engineering and renewable energy.

Conference Tracks

Potential Tracks

Track 1

Power Systems, Renewable Energy & Electrification

Advances in sustainable, resilient, and intelligent energy systems — from modern power networks and renewable integration to storage and grid reliability.

Smart Grids Renewable Integration Energy Storage EV Infrastructure Grid Reliability High-Voltage Engineering
Track 2

Artificial Intelligence, Computing & Digital Transformation

Emerging technologies driving the digital era — AI, intelligent computing, data-driven innovation, cybersecurity, and transformative digital solutions.

Machine Learning & NLP Autonomous Systems Edge Computing Data Science Cybersecurity & Blockchain Digital Twins
Track 3

Resilient Infrastructure, Environmental Policy & Climate Science

Sustainable and climate-responsive approaches to infrastructure, environmental governance, and scientific innovation for global challenges.

Sustainable Materials Structural Health Climate-Resilient Design Green Policy Circular Economy Hydrological Modeling
